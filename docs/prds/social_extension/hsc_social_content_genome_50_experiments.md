# HSC Social Content Genome — First 50 Experiments

**Purpose:** Seed PB19/PB21 with an initial experiment library for Houston Sign Crafters.  
**Primary platforms:** Instagram Reels + TikTok  
**Default audience:** Houston/Texas business owners, franchise operators, developers, GCs, property/facility teams.  
**Rule:** These are experiments, not a 50-post calendar. PB19 should select from them based on available HSC assets, current evidence, and PB21 learnings.

---

# 1. Genome Fields

Every post should record:

- `format`
- `hook_family`
- `industry`
- `product`
- `proof_type`
- `human_presence`
- `length_bucket`
- `cta_family`
- `editing_style`
- `audio_type`
- `geography`
- `primary_metric`
- `hypothesis`

The goal is to learn **which combinations generate qualified commercial attention**, not simply what gets views.

---

# 2. Initial Portfolio Mix

Start approximately:

- 30% transformations/reveals
- 20% pricing/economics
- 20% mistakes/education
- 15% fabrication/install/process
- 10% local/business-owner commentary
- 5% humor/experiments

At least half of posts should use real completed HSC work.

# 4. First 12 I Would Run

Do not start by randomly selecting 50. I would seed PB19 with these first 12 because they cover distinct hypotheses while using HSC's strongest assets:

1. #1 — $X restaurant transformation
2. #4 — Rendering vs reality
3. #11 — Why this cost $X
4. #14 — Cheap quote teardown
5. #19 — Landlord approval mistake
6. #21 — Wrong sign size / distance
7. #24 — Halo vs front-lit
8. #29 — How channel letters are made
9. #33 — Crane install POV
10. #37 — Day with install team
11. #40 — Free mockup challenge
12. #50 — Sign rescue / expensive mistake

This gives PB21 variation across:

- transformation vs education vs process,
- price vs fear vs curiosity vs direct-response hooks,
- owner vs crew vs no-face formats,
- direct lead CTA vs no CTA.

# 5. Controlled Test Pairs

PB21 should turn some of the above into controlled comparisons.

## Pair A — Price vs transformation

Same restaurant project/edit.

**A:** “This restaurant spent $14,000 on this sign.”  
**B:** “Watch this storefront completely change.”

Primary metric: qualified engagement rate.

## Pair B — Fear vs checklist

Same permitting subject.

**A:** “You can pay for a sign your landlord rejects.”  
**B:** “3 landlord sign rules to check before ordering.”

## Pair C — Founder vs voiceover

Same footage and hook, Rameel on camera vs voiceover-only.

## Pair D — 15s vs 30s

Same story compressed into two durations.

## Pair E — Direct CTA vs no CTA

Same project content.

**A:** “Send your storefront + logo and we'll mock it up.”  
**B:** no sales CTA.

Measure whether CTA hurts distribution enough to outweigh conversion.

## Pair F — Real price vs price range

Only on projects where HSC approves disclosure.

# 6. PB19 Selection Rules

Each day PB19 should choose a portfolio rather than three copies of the same idea:

### Post A — proven lane

Use a current PB21 winner or high-confidence format.

### Post B — controlled experiment

Change one meaningful variable.

### Optional Post C — exploration

New format/trend with high visual fit but limited HSC evidence.

This is analogous to an explore/exploit system:

- 60% proven,
- 30% adjacent tests,
- 10% novel experiments.

---

# 7. CTAs to Rotate

Avoid putting `GET A QUOTE` on every post.

CTA genome:

- no CTA
- “Would you have changed anything?”
- “Save this before you order a sign.”
- “Send us your storefront + logo.”
- “Comment the city and I’ll tell you what permits usually matter.” (only if team can respond accurately)
- “DM us your storefront.”
- “Opening a location? Send the plans.”
- “Know someone opening a business in Houston? Send this to them.”
- “Which version would you choose?”

PB21 should measure conversion/distribution tradeoffs by CTA.

---

# 8. HSC Content Capture System

PB19 gets much better if HSC standardizes capture during every job.

For each installation, collect:

1. 5s exterior before, locked shot.
2. 5s close-up of old/blank area.
3. 5s truck/crew arrival.
4. 5s sign on ground.
5. 5s lift/crane action.
6. 5s fastening/wiring detail.
7. 5s wide reveal from same angle as before.
8. 5s walking/panning reveal.
9. nighttime exterior if illuminated.
10. one 10s installer explanation of anything unusual.

At shop:

- returns being formed,
- backs/faces,
- LEDs,
- trimcap,
- paint,
- test lighting,
- packaging/loading.

If you operationalize this, the social agent stops being starved for footage.

---

# 9. What PB21 Should Learn First

The first month should answer these questions:

1. Price hook vs transformation hook?
2. Restaurant vs general commercial?
3. Before/after vs process?
4. Human face vs no face?
5. Rameel vs installer/fabricator?
6. 15–25s vs 26–40s?
7. Direct quote CTA vs soft/no CTA?
8. Raw phone footage vs polished cinematic?
9. Specific Houston/local hook vs generic?
10. Completed project proof vs educational drawing/permit content?
11. Which product produces the most qualified engagement?
12. Which genome produces actual pipeline per 1,000 views?

Those answers matter more than follower count.
